# Food_Waste_Management

A web platform connecting hotels with leftover food to NGOs helping the needy.

Both hotels and NGOs register/login with their name and details.

After login:

Hotels can view and fill a food chart to submit leftover food details.

NGOs can view a list of hotels and their available food charts.

NGOs can:

Send a request to a hotel for the food they want.

Contact the hotel directly if needed.

Hotels receive the NGO requests in their dashboard and can approve them.

Once approved, the food is sent to the NGO for distribution to the hungry.

Helps reduce food wastage and solve hunger by ensuring surplus food reaches those in need.
